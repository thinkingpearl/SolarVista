Formula Reference

Annual generation is derived from usable area, effective annual irradiance and system efficiency:

E ≈ A × Heff × η × PR

A = usable area, Heff = annual irradiance adjusted for tilt, orientation and shading, η = panel efficiency, PR = performance ratio.
