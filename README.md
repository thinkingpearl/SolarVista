# ☀️ SolarVista — AI Powered Rooftop Mapping

**Making rooftop solar adoption smarter, simpler, and more accessible.**

SolarVista is a solar-energy potential estimation platform designed to help users evaluate the feasibility of installing solar panels on rooftops using geospatial data and satellite imagery. By analyzing rooftop area, shading, and other relevant parameters, SolarVista aims to provide insights into potential solar power generation, installation costs, and estimated savings.

## 🌍 Overview

The transition to solar energy is often hindered by the difficulty of assessing rooftop suitability, estimating energy generation, and understanding the financial benefits of installation.

SolarVista aims to bridge this gap by providing a data-driven approach to rooftop solar assessment, empowering individuals and communities to make informed decisions about adopting solar energy.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Rooftop Mapping:** Identify and analyze rooftop areas using geospatial and satellite data.
- **Solar Potential Estimation:** Estimate the potential for solar energy generation based on rooftop characteristics.
- **Shadow Analysis:** Consider shading effects that may influence solar panel efficiency.
- **Energy Generation Forecasting:** Provide estimates of potential electricity generation.
- **Cost Estimation:** Help users understand the approximate cost of solar panel installation.
- **Savings Analysis:** Estimate potential electricity bill savings and the financial benefits of adopting solar energy.
- **Data-Driven Insights:** Present relevant information to support informed solar installation decisions.

*Feature availability depends on the current implementation of the prototype.*
